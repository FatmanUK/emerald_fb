Emerald FB

A reimplementation of Fuzzball MUCK 7 in Go, aiming for behaviour
compatibility: existing MUF programs, MPI descriptions and .db worlds
should work unchanged.

Emerald FB is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Fuzzball MUCK
project. "FB" is attribution, the abbreviation upstream uses itself.

Fuzzball MUCK is the work of the Fuzzball development team and of
everyone who worked on TinyMUCK before it. Emerald owes all of its
behaviour to theirs; where the two disagree, theirs is the original.

  Fuzzball     https://www.fuzzball.org/
  Emerald FB   https://github.com/FatmanUK/emerald_fb
